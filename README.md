# 2025

## 📍 Development of a Framework for Location-Based Messaging with Augmented Reality

**Research Domain:** Augmented Reality • Human–Computer Interaction • Mobile Computing • Software Engineering

This research presents a cross-platform framework that integrates **location-based messaging** with **Augmented Reality (AR)** to enhance contextual communication and reduce the likelihood of users forgetting important location-specific tasks. While conventional location-based messaging applications primarily rely on notifications, this work enriches the user experience by incorporating immersive AR visualizations, making messages more engaging and context-aware.

The developed framework enables users to create and receive messages that are triggered at predefined geographic locations and visualized through augmented reality. To evaluate its effectiveness, a comprehensive usability study involving **96 participants** was conducted using two widely adopted user experience metrics: the **System Usability Scale (SUS)** and the **Net Promoter Score (NPS)**.

The proposed framework achieved a **System Usability Scale (SUS) score of 71.25** and favorable **Net Promoter Scores (NPS) exceeding 20**, demonstrating good usability and positive user acceptance. As an open-source framework, the system provides a foundation for future research and development in immersive, context-aware communication technologies and emerging AR platforms.

## 2024

### 🎮 Mobile Legends Win Rate Prediction and Team Recommendation Using Switched Hero Roles

**Research Domain:** Artificial Intelligence • Machine Learning • Recommender Systems • Game Analytics

This research investigates the impact of **non-traditional hero role assignments** on match outcomes in *Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB)*. While previous studies primarily focused on conventional team compositions, this work explores how swapping heroes into unconventional roles influences team performance and victory probability.

A comprehensive dataset comprising **67 MLBB heroes** was constructed by modifying their traditional gameplay roles. Twelve regression algorithms combined with five feature selection techniques were evaluated to predict team win rates under different role-switching scenarios. Based on the best-performing prediction model, an intelligent team recommendation system was developed to optimize team composition.

Among all evaluated models, **Light Gradient Boosting Machine (LightGBM) with AdaBoost**, followed by **LightGBM** and **Random Forest with AdaBoost**, achieved the highest predictive performance. Simulation experiments demonstrated that the top recommended team achieved a **94% win rate (47 wins out of 50 matches)**, highlighting the potential of unconventional hero role assignments for improving competitive gameplay strategies.
